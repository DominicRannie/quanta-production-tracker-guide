# Content types

A piece's content type decides which steps it gets and how far before publication each one falls. The types in the tracker today:

| Type | Stream | What a piece gets |
|---|---|---|
| **Articles** | Articles | the full article workflow: pitch and writer steps, draft in, first and second edit, the lede-art chain, image research and secondary art, fact check, top edit, copyedit, layout, staging, final check, publish, social and archive. About thirty steps, more when a graphic or a photo shoot is ticked |
| **Articles – Image Gallery** | Articles | a reduced set: top edit, fact check, copyedit, art requests and Mural review, social, publish |
| **Articles – Transformation** | Articles | the maths-and-AI blog: staging, social, publish |
| **Articles – Social Exclusive** | Articles | no steps yet — the workflow is to come |
| **The Joy of Why** | Podcasts | sixteen steps from interview research to YouTube, six months ahead |
| **The Quanta Podcast** | Podcasts | interview, promo, audio delivered and approved, staging, social, publish |
| **Video** | Video | fourteen steps from contractor paperwork and script reviews to shorts release |
| **Newsletters** | Newsletters | steps to come |

Two ticks on a piece add steps: **Graphics needed** adds *Commission infographic* and alerts the art editor and graphics designer; **Photo shoot** adds *Commission photo shoot*.

Some types still show their offsets as pending; their steps appear *Undated* until an admin fills them in on the [step templates](../admin/step-templates.md).
