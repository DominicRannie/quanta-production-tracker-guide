# Who does what on a piece

Each piece names its people, and the tracker uses those names to decide who owns each step. Set them once and the steps follow.

## The people fields

| Field | Whose steps it owns |
|---|---|
| **Editor** | the editorial steps: draft in, first and second edit, fact check, receiving the art request form |
| **Hosts** | the host steps on podcast episodes |
| **Top editor** | top edit, and the final check on publication day |
| **Fact checker** | reserved for a separate fact checker; today the fact check step goes to the editor |
| **Copy editor** | copyedit (Roberta Klarreich when blank) |
| **Image researcher** | image research, secondary art in, secondary art cropped (Arleen Santana when blank) |
| **Staging and publishing** | article on staging, publish, and the post-publication steps — assets archived, homepage screenshot, syndication (Arleen Santana when blank) |
| **Graphics designer** | commission infographic, when the piece needs one (Mark Belan when blank) |

Leave a field blank and its steps go to the team's usual person, shown in brackets. Steps with no field at all always go to the team: the lede-art chain, photo shoots, the Mural review and layout to Samuel Velasco; the social steps to Kit Sudol and Simone Barr. You only need to fill a field when the usual person is not doing it.

## Change a person

Change the name in the field. Every open step for that role moves to the new person and they get a Slack message for each. Steps already done stay with whoever did them.

!!! tip "Change the piece, not the steps"
    If you reassign one step by hand but the piece still names the old person for that role, the step goes back to them the next time the piece's dates or people change. To hand over a role, change it on the piece. To hand over a single job as a one-off, see [Reassign a step](../steps/reassign-a-step.md).
