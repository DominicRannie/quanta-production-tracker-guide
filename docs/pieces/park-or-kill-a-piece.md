# Park or kill a piece

Some pieces stall; some die. The tracker has one field for both, and it leaves everything else alone.

## Put a piece on hold

Open the piece and set **Status override** to **On hold**. The piece's status shows *On hold*, it drops out of everyone's morning list, and its steps stop sending alerts. Nothing is deleted: the publish date, the steps and their owners all stay as they were. When the piece comes back, clear the override and, if the date has moved, change the publish date — the steps re-date themselves.

## Kill a piece

Set **Status override** to **Killed**. Same effect, with a final-sounding label. A killed piece keeps its history, so the record of what was done stays in the tracker.

## Why not clear the date?

Clearing the publish date un-dates every open step but keeps them on the piece, and the piece drops back to *Pitched*. That is right for a piece that has gone back to being a pitch; it is wrong for one that is simply waiting. Use *On hold* for waiting.
