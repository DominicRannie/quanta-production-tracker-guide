# Your day

A normal day with the tracker takes a few minutes, in three moments.

## 8:30 — the morning list

At 8:30 New York every weekday, the tracker sends you a Slack direct message if anything on your list needs attention: steps due today, steps due back today, anything overdue, and — for editors — writers' drafts that were due on pieces not yet scheduled. Each line is a link to the step. If nothing is due, no message arrives.

## Through the day — My work

Open [**My work**](https://airtable.com/appFlJWmSk0RDIf1U/pbdMlP776N9XQeREM/pagH9V6ux14ZXhxhl){target=_blank} and find your name. Your steps are listed soonest first, with the piece they belong to and their status: *Overdue*, *Due soon* (within three days), *Upcoming* or *Undated*. The tabs along the top cut the list by track; *Overdue* shows only what is late. When you finish one, open it and tick **Done**. That is all the tracker needs; the piece's status and the next person's list update on their own.

If a step arrives on your list during the day — a new piece was scheduled, or someone passed a step to you — Slack tells you at the time.

## When you own a piece

Editors have one more habit. When a pitch becomes a commission, give it its [publish date](../pieces/schedule-a-piece.md) and name its [people](../pieces/who-does-what.md). From then on the tracker runs the calendar for you.
