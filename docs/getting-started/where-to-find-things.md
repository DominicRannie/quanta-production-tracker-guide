# Where to find things

The tracker is an Airtable interface called **Production tracker**. It has four screens, and every piece opens into its own page.

| Screen | What it is for |
|---|---|
| [**My work**](https://airtable.com/appFlJWmSk0RDIf1U/pbdMlP776N9XQeREM/pagH9V6ux14ZXhxhl){target=_blank} | Your own steps, soonest first. This is where most people spend their day. |
| [**Timeline**](https://airtable.com/appFlJWmSk0RDIf1U/pbdMlP776N9XQeREM/pag2Lck56OEnw9CxY){target=_blank} | Every piece on a calendar by publish date — the production calendar. |
| [**Pipeline**](https://airtable.com/appFlJWmSk0RDIf1U/pbdMlP776N9XQeREM/pagTU2JvGpMKJK4jc){target=_blank} | Every piece grouped by status, from pitched to published. |
| [**Art requests**](https://airtable.com/appFlJWmSk0RDIf1U/pbdMlP776N9XQeREM/pagcK8rOZFtdfgdEn){target=_blank} | The art team's view: every request and the images asked for. |

## A piece's page

Click any piece on Timeline or Pipeline and it opens. The top shows its name, type, publish date, status and people. Below are its steps by track, its art request, and its notes and links. Almost everything you do to a piece — set the date, name the fact checker, put it on hold — you do here.

<span class="screenshot-placeholder">Screenshot to add: a piece's page with the people fields and the steps list</span>

## A step's page

Click a step anywhere and it opens too: owner, due, due back, the Done box, a notes field, and a place for the draft link or file.

!!! note "Where is the backlog?"
    Pitched pieces — no date yet — show at the top of **Pipeline** under *Pitched*. A dedicated backlog page grouped by subject area is coming once the pitch flow is agreed.
