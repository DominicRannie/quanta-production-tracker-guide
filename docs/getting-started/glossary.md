# Glossary

**Piece** — one thing to be published: an article, episode, video or newsletter. One row in the tracker.

**Slug** — a piece's short working name, in capitals (`RNA WORLD`). Steps are named after it: `RNA WORLD · Fact check`.

**Content type** — the kind of piece: Articles, Video, The Joy of Why, The Quanta Podcast, Articles – Image Gallery, Articles – Transformation, Newsletters, and so on. The type decides which steps a piece gets.

**Pitch / Pitched** — a piece with no publish date yet. It sits in the backlog and has no steps.

**Commissioned** — a piece with a publish date and nothing editorial ticked yet.

**Publish date** — the day the piece goes out. Every step's date is counted back from it.

**Draft due** — the writer's deadline, set by the editor when known. The *Draft in* step takes this date.

**Commission date** — stamped automatically the first time a piece gets a publish date.

**Step** — one job on one piece, with an owner, a due date and a Done box.

**Due / Due back** — when a step must be started or delivered, and when the work returns to the editor (9 am New York on the due-back day).

**Working days** — Monday to Friday. Editorial steps are counted in working days, so a weekend never falls inside a window.

**Track** — a team's lane through a piece: Editorial, Lede art, Secondary art, Graphics, Production, Audience. Each has a status on the piece.

**Status** — the piece's overall position: On hold or Killed if overridden; else Published; else the latest editorial step done; else Commissioned or Pitched.

**Status override** — an editor's manual setting of *On hold* or *Killed*. While set, the piece sends no alerts.

**Next step** — on a piece, the open step or steps due soonest, with the date.

**Art request** — the editor's request for images on a piece, with one line per image wanted (an *item*).

**Graphics needed** — a tick on the piece meaning it wants an infographic. Ticking it adds the *Commission infographic* step and tells the art editor and graphics designer.

**Embargo date** — the day an embargo lifts; the piece cannot publish before it.

**Step template** — the admin's definition of which steps a content type gets, who owns them and how far before publication they fall.
