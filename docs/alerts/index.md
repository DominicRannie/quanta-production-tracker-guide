# Slack alerts

The tracker talks to you through Slack direct messages from **Quanta Production Tracker**. There are four kinds. Each is short, and each line is a link straight to the step or the piece.

## The morning list

Every weekday at **8:30 New York**, one message if you have anything due: steps due today, steps due back today, anything overdue (with how many days), and — for editors — writers' drafts due on pieces not yet scheduled. Nothing due, no message. Pieces on hold, killed or published are left out.

## New steps on your list

When a piece is scheduled and the tracker creates its steps, everyone who received some gets one message listing them with their dates.

## A step passed to you

When a step's owner changes — by hand, or because someone on the piece changed — the new owner is told at once.

## A graphic is wanted

When **Graphics needed** is ticked on a piece, the art editor and the graphics designer are told.

## If you get nothing

Alerts go to the Slack member ID on your row in **People**. If yours is blank, you get no messages, and the run notes who was missed. Ask an admin to fill it in — see [People and Slack](../admin/people-and-slack.md).

!!! note "Changing the wording"
    The text of every alert is written in the automation itself, not in code, so an admin can change it. See [Change the wording of an alert](../admin/change-alert-wording.md).
