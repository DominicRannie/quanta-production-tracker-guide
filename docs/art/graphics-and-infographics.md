# Graphics and infographics

A piece that needs an infographic is one tick away from the right people knowing.

## Tick Graphics needed

Open the piece and tick **Graphics needed**. Two things happen at once. The *Commission infographic* step is added to the piece, owned by the piece's **Graphics designer** (Mark Belan when the field is blank) and dated six weeks before publication. And the art editor and the graphics designer each get a Slack message naming the piece, its editor and its publish date, with a link.

<span class="screenshot-placeholder">Screenshot to add: the Graphics needed tick and the Commission infographic step on a piece</span>

Unticking it later removes nothing and sends nothing; delete the step by hand if the graphic is dropped.

## Or ask for it on the art request

Adding an item of type **Graphic or figure** to the piece's [art request](request-art.md) ticks **Graphics needed** for you, with the same result.

## A different designer

Name the person in the piece's **Graphics designer** field before ticking, or change it afterwards — the step and the alert follow the field.
