# Add your own step

Sometimes a piece needs a job the template doesn't know about: a second fact-check pass, a legal read, a translation. Add it as a step of your own.

1. On [**My work**](https://airtable.com/appFlJWmSk0RDIf1U/pbdMlP776N9XQeREM/pagH9V6ux14ZXhxhl){target=_blank}, add a new step.
2. Give it a **Name**, link it to the piece under **Content**, set an **Owner**, a **Due** date and, if useful, a **Track**.

It behaves like any other step: it appears on the owner's list, in their morning message, and ticking it works the same way. The one difference is that the tracker never re-dates, re-owns or removes a step you added yourself — it is yours.

!!! tip
    Name it the way the standard steps are named, `SLUG · Job`, so it sorts with the others.
