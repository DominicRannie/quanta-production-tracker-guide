# Tick a step

When a job is finished, tick it. That single action keeps the whole piece's status honest.

1. Open the step from [My work](https://airtable.com/appFlJWmSk0RDIf1U/pbdMlP776N9XQeREM/pagH9V6ux14ZXhxhl){target=_blank} or from the piece's page.
2. Tick **Done**.

The tracker stamps **Done on** with today's date, moves the piece's status for that track to this step, and if this was the *Publish* step, marks the piece *Published*. Your morning list no longer mentions it.

## Untick it

Ticked by mistake? Untick it. The track's status rolls back to the previous done step, and the step returns to your list.

## Drafts, links and notes

A step has a **Draft link**, a **Draft file** and **Notes**. Use them for what the step produced — the draft that came in, the fact-check memo — so the next person finds it on the step rather than in Slack.

!!! note "Steps you can't date"
    *First edit* and *Second edit* come without dates: the editor sets them when the draft arrives. Until then they show *Undated*.
