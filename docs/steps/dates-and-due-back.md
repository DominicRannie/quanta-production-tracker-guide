# Dates and due back

Every standard step is dated from the piece's publish date, so the calendar is always consistent.

## Due and due back

**Due** is when a step must start or be delivered. **Due back** is when the work returns to the editor, and it is always 9 am New York on the due-back day. Editorial steps are counted in **working days**, Monday to Friday, so a window never swallows a weekend.

| Step | Due | Back |
|---|---|---|
| Fact check, Top edit | 9 working days before publication | 5 working days later |
| Copyedit | 2 working days before | 1 working day later |
| Final check | the day of publication | — |
| Publish | the day | — |
| Draft in | 6 weeks before, or the piece's **Draft due** when set | — |
| Art steps | calendar weeks before publication, per step (lede idea −4, lede in −3, Mural review −2, secondary art cropped −1) | image research: 14 days |
| Social copy, social scheduled | 3 days and 1 day before | — |
| Archive, homepage screenshot, syndication, IG story | after publication; undated until the team dates them | — |

!!! example
    Publish date Monday 14 December. Nine working days back is Tuesday 1 December, so fact check and top edit are due then; five working days on is Tuesday 8 December, 9 am, when they come back.

## When the date moves

Change the publish date and every open step is re-dated the same way. Done steps keep their dates.

## Undated steps

A step shows *Undated* when the tracker has no offset for it (first and second edit, which the editor dates) or when the piece has no publish date. Set the date on the step by hand if you need one.

## Overdue

A step whose due date has passed shows *Overdue* on your list and in your morning message, with how many days late. Tick it or re-date it; it does not go away on its own.
