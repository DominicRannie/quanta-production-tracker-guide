# Reassign a step

To hand one job to someone else, change the step's **Owner**. The new owner gets a Slack message straight away with the step, its dates and a link.

## Two ways, two meanings

**Change the Owner on the step** for a one-off: this fact check goes to a colleague while the usual person is away. Every current owner is told.

**Change the person on the piece** to hand over a role: a new copy editor for the whole piece. All their open steps move at once. See [Who does what](../pieces/who-does-what.md).

!!! warning "A step reassigned by hand can bounce back"
    If the step's role is one the piece names — editor, top editor, fact checker, copy editor, image researcher, staging — and the piece still names the old person, the step returns to them the next time the piece's dates or people change. For anything more than a one-off, change the piece.
